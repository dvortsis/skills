## Questions
### What Is the UAT Page?
The page where you check dashboard numbers during user acceptance testing (UAT), the last check before a dashboard goes live. It has three tabs, used in order: **1 · Parameters**, **2 · Testing** and **3 · Sign-off**.

### What Do I Need Before I Start?
Your legacy report or extract for each dashboard. Never estimate a value from memory.

### Why Can't I Click Record Result?
**Record result** stays disabled until every value for the scenario is entered on **1 · Parameters**.

### What Does the Result Form Ask?
Whether the dashboard matched, the dashboard value, the report value and the report you used. Pick a report already logged or add a new one.

### What Happens When a Test Fails or Is Blocked?
Pick a severity (how much it matters) and add a note. That opens an issue in **Issues found**. The business intelligence (BI) team reviews it and marks it FIX REQUIRED, NOT A DEFECT or DEFERRED TO PHASE 2, with the reason.

### Can I Change a Value I Already Entered?
Yes. Click **Reopen to update** on the completed card. The card keeps its history.

### How Do I Know What Is Done?
Completed cards and passed tests turn gray.

### When Do I Sign Off?
Sign each dashboard off on **3 · Sign-off**. The tab warns you when tests have not passed or issues are still open.

## Get Help
Ask the BI team member who runs your testing session.
