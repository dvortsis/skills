## Why This Is Changing
Every dashboard is checked against your own numbers before it goes live. The team writes each test scenario from a business question, and you supply the real values and compare the results with your own report during user acceptance testing (UAT).

## What Changes for You
You enter values, record results and sign off on one UAT page with three tabs, used in order:
- **1 · Parameters**: enter the values each scenario needs, in plain language.
- **2 · Testing**: follow each scenario's steps and click **Record result**.
- **3 · Sign-off**: sign each dashboard off.

## What to Do
1. Find your legacy report or extract for each dashboard. Never estimate a value from memory.
2. Enter every value on **1 · Parameters** before testing starts. **Record result** stays disabled until you do.
3. For a Fail or Blocked result, pick a severity (how much it matters) and add a note.
