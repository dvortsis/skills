## Instructions
Answer each question, then check the answer and read why each option is right or wrong. Score one point per correct answer.

## Questions
### Q1
You clicked a payer bar. How do you see all payers again?
A. Refresh the browser  B. Click the same bar again  C. Close the report
Answer: B
Why: Clicking a selected bar again clears the selection.
Why not A: Refreshing reloads the page but can keep the selection.
Why not C: Closing the report loses your place and your filters.

### Q2
Which filter limits every number on the page to your work?
A. Region  B. Chart color  C. Sort order
Answer: A
Why: The region filter applies to the whole report.
Why not B: Chart color changes how the bars look, not which numbers show.
Why not C: Sort order changes the order of the rows, not which numbers show.

### Q3
Two reasons tie on claim count. Which do you work first?
A. The alphabetically first  B. The one with more denied dollars  C. Either
Answer: B
Why: Denied dollars show where one fix recovers the most revenue.
Why not A: The name of a reason says nothing about its value.
Why not C: Picking at random can leave the bigger dollar amount for later.
