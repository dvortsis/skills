## Who It's For
Billing specialists who work denials and team leads who assign them.

## What You Can Do
* See denied claims and dollars for your region.
* Find the payer and reason that need attention first.
* Save a view that opens with your filters.

## Steps
1. Open the report from the team bookmarks.
2. Set the region and month filters.
3. Click the longest payer bar to filter the reasons chart.
4. Pick the top reason by denied dollars.
5. Save your filters as a custom view.

## Worked Example
A specialist in the West region sets the filters, clicks the payer with the most denied dollars and sees that missing authorization tops the list. They work those claims first.

## Common Mistakes
* Leaving a payer selected and reading the wrong totals. Click the bar again to clear it.
* Comparing averages of rates across regions. Compare the region totals instead.
