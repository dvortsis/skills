## When to Use
Use this when the numbers look wrong or a chart shows fewer bars than expected.

## Steps
1. Look at the filter bar for any value that is not "All".
2. Click any highlighted bar again to clear its selection.
3. Click Reset in the toolbar to restore the default view.
4. Reopen your saved custom view if you use one.

## Check Your Work
The headline numbers match the totals on the report home page.
