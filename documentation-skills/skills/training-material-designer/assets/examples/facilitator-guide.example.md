## Session Overview
A 45-minute live session. Learners follow along in the report with their own login.

## Learning Objectives
* Filter the report to their own work.
* Choose the denial reason to work first and explain why.
* Save and reopen a custom view.

## Agenda
| Time | Activity | Path |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Why this report matters | All |
| 5-20 | Module 1: Read the page | Foundation |
| 20-35 | Module 2: Pick the next reason | Standard |
| 35-45 | Transfer scenario and wrap-up | All |

## Modules
### Module 1: Read the Page
Walk the page top to bottom: headline numbers, trend, drivers, detail. Demonstrate the region filter.
Check: what changes when you set the region filter? Answer: every number on the page, because the filter applies to the whole report.

### Module 2: Pick the Next Reason
Demonstrate clicking a payer bar, then reading the reasons chart. Learners repeat it for their region.
Check: two reasons tie on count. Which comes first? Answer: the one with more denied dollars.

## Practice Scenario
Learners filter to their region, pick a reason and post it in the chat with one sentence on why.

## Transfer Scenario
It is Monday and denied dollars rose for a payer nobody watches. Ask: what are your next three actions? Expected: filter to that payer, read the top reason, tell your lead.

## Materials
* Report link and the quick start.
* Practice login with synthetic data.

## Follow-up
Send the quick start and the video after the session. Check saved views at the next huddle.
