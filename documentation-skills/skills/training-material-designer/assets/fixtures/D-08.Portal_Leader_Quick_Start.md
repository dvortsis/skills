## Who It's For
Leaders who approve the wireframe design and give sign-off on each dashboard at the end of user acceptance testing (UAT).

## What You Can Do
* Approve the design once your team's questions are covered.
* Keep reporting and workflow needs separate.
* Give sign-off when every result is recorded.

![Illustration, not a live screenshot, of how version labels move from drafts to a final version](figs/fig_version_labels.png)

## Steps
1. In the **User Feedback Matrix**, check that **Awaiting decision** is empty and read the reasons for declined items.
2. Raise anything important before approval; approval locks the design.
3. Plan three to six hours a week for your team during review phases.
4. On the UAT page, open **3 · Sign-off** and check **Tests passed** and open issues.
5. Click **Sign off** for each ready dashboard. Agree any exception with the project team first.

## Worked Example
At the end of UAT, one dashboard has an open issue. The director signs off the other two and waits for the retest.

## Common Mistakes
* Signing off past a warning without an agreed exception.
* Adding new requests after approval. Late requests move to a later phase.
