# Learning method (apply to every format)

1. **Task first.** Open with the job the learner has to do and the decision or action the training enables. Write objectives as observable actions: find, choose, submit, explain, fix. Never "understand" or "know".
2. **Mental model before clicks.** Say why it matters and how the thing is organized (for a report: headline numbers, then trend, then drivers, then detail; for a process: trigger, steps, hand-offs, done) before any procedure.
3. **Worked example, then practice.** Show one realistic scenario end to end, then have the learner do a similar one with different values.
4. **Retrieval every 2–3 minutes or slides.** Short "what would you do / what does this mean" checks, each with the answer and why each option is right or wrong. Test decisions, not trivia.
5. **Three paths.** Foundation (get in, read, find), Standard (the everyday task end to end), Advanced (exceptions, troubleshooting, judgment calls). Label which path each part serves; one deliverable usually targets one path.
6. **Transfer.** End with a new situation the training didn't show ("It's Monday and the queue doubled…") and the next three actions.
7. **Spaced follow-up.** Name the follow-up: a job aid at the desk, a knowledge check a week later, a huddle question, and the follow-up evaluation 30 to 60 days after training.
8. **High signal, accessible.** Plain language (aim for grade 8), one idea per step, sentences under 20 words, visuals paired with a one-line explanation, readable type, never color alone to carry meaning, captions or transcript for video.

## Choosing a format
| Need | Format |
|---|---|
| Teach a task once, async, to many people | video-script (2–5 min) |
| First day with a tool or report | quick-start (one page) |
| Do a rare or error-prone task correctly | job-aid (one task, at the desk) |
| Live or virtual session | facilitator-guide (+ deck) |
| Confirm learning or certify | knowledge-check |
| Rollout or change announcement to leaders | deck via slide-deck |
| Permanent home for all of the above | training-page via confluence-page-builder (optional) |
