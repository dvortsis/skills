# Subject facts table (build before writing)

Write `<subject>.facts.md` with one row per fact: `| Fact | Source |`. Fill only what a source states; anything else becomes an open question for the user. Pick the profile that fits; use more than one when needed.

## Report or dashboard
* Business question it answers and who uses it.
* Where to open it and how to get access; refresh time.
* Layout from top to bottom (headline numbers, trends, drivers, detail) and what each part shows.
* Filters and which parts they affect; how selections filter other views and how to clear them; reset.
* Metric definitions and their certified source; what any alert color means (state it exactly as the source does).
* Saved views, subscriptions, exports or alerts, but only if the source confirms they exist.
* Where to ask questions and report data issues.

## Application or system
* Purpose, users and roles; how to log in and get access.
* The 3–5 tasks people do most, each with exact screen and button names from the source or the user's screenshots.
* Required fields, validations and common error messages with their fixes.
* What happens next (hand-offs, notifications, turnaround).
* Support path and escalation.

## Process, policy or procedure
* Trigger, owner, roles, inputs and outputs.
* Steps in order, decision points and exceptions.
* Deadlines, controls and compliance requirements, quoted from the policy.
* What "done" looks like and how it is checked.
* Where the official policy or SOP lives (link it).

## Rollout or change
* What changes, for whom, when and why.
* What people must do differently, and by when.
* What stays the same.
* Training and support offered, and where to get help.
